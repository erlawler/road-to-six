# Road to Six: 90-second reviewer guide

**Prepared:** September 7, 2026
**Owner:** Eric Lawler
**Purpose:** Show product judgment, a usable forecast, and inspectable evidence in one short review.

## Start with the evidence boundary

Public production is Sites version 16 at main commit `57b170f`. The September candidate is local and undeployed. Use the [candidate review](flagship-candidate-2026-09-07.md) to identify which behaviors are actually validated before demonstrating them. The July scorecard and screenshots are historical evidence. No human research or measured adoption is claimed.

## The demonstration

| Time | Show | Explain |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 15 seconds | Product hero and ownership | I led the scope, data and AI boundaries, acceptance criteria, and release decisions. Codex accelerated implementation and review. The user job is to understand a forecast and its limitations. |
| 15 to 35 seconds | One matchup and one participation control | Change one assumption. Read the original and resulting probability. In the candidate, show the final percentage-point change and reconciled contributions. The controls are illustrative sensitivity assumptions, not medical reports or measured player effects. |
| 35 to 50 seconds | Source freshness and market coverage | Name the football validation date, market retrieval time, and sportsbook count. A single-book price is thin coverage; a successful refresh does not make every source current. |
| 50 to 65 seconds | Deterministic result and a previously validated AI receipt | Code owns the number. Production demonstrates exact evidence reproduction. The candidate experimentally lets AI select and order 1 to 3 approved what-changed facts; the application owns every displayed fact. Incremental user value is unmeasured. |
| 65 to 80 seconds | Model audit | The historical blend improved on football-only Elo but did not beat the market baseline. The hand-set participation controls and fixed uncertainty band have a different evidence boundary from the historical backtest. |
| 80 to 90 seconds | One decision and the prospective study | Point to the cost cutoff, fallback, or rejected paid-data scope. Explain that the next product decision will use five voluntary comparisons of deterministic context and AI-selected context. |

Use an existing sanitized, version-matched receipt for a cost-free demonstration. This script does not authorize a new paid call. If no candidate AI receipt exists, show the deterministic path and label candidate AI evidence as pending. Do not present a fixture, prototype, or historical output as a current live result.
